5. Cookies and tracking

This website uses technically necessary local storage and, after your explicit consent, Google Analytics 4 for reach measurement.

For technically necessary functions, only local storage on your device is used: your chosen display setting (light or dark theme) is stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage), and a service worker caches website content to make the site faster and available offline (Progressive Web App). This data remains on your device, contains no personal data, and is not transmitted to us or to third parties. No consent is required for this, as it is strictly necessary storage within the meaning of Section 25(2) of the German TDDDG. You can delete this data at any time via your browser settings.

If you click “Accept” in the cookie notice, Google Analytics 4 is loaded. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Usage data (e.g. pages visited, approximate location information, browser and device information, and truncated or otherwise processed IP addresses) may be processed to statistically evaluate the use of the website. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG. Processing by Google LLC in the USA cannot be ruled out; Google relies, among other things, on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

After your consent, Google Analytics sets cookies on your device: the “_ga” cookie and a cookie with the prefix “_ga_” (on this website “_ga_ZKB4JPM2LK”). Both are used to recognise your browser and are stored for up to two years.

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future: click “Cookie settings” in the footer of the website and choose “Only necessary”. The cookies set by Google Analytics are then deleted and further collection on this device is disabled.
